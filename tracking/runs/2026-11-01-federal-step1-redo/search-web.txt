The Software Engineering and Data Automation Specialist will work within the Information Systems team of the Office of Administration and Technology to support the research and operational work of the United States Sentencing Commission. The incumbent will design, develop, integrate, and maintain applications, data-processing workflows, data warehouse solutions, and analytics systems, with an emphasis on document and data extraction, processing, transformation, validation, integration, and automation.
The position encompasses both modernization projects and ongoing support of existing systems. Assignments may include leading or supporting document extraction, data transformation, database integration, reporting, and application maintenance, with responsibilities varying according to project priorities, operational needs, and team expertise. The incumbent will collaborate with technical staff and subject-matter experts to develop shared knowledge of the Commission's data, business rules, and systems. The ideal candidate will be passionate about data and technology, possess strong analytical and problem-solving skills, and enjoy developing practical solutions to complex data and automation challenges.
The incumbent will:

  * Analyze business and user requirements and design, develop, implement, and maintain software solutions supporting data collection, document processing, data integration, business intelligence, and analytics.
  * Design and develop Python applications and automated pipelines to ingest and process PDFs, scanned documents, databases, and other structured and unstructured data sources.
  * Develop and enhance OCR, text parsing, regular-expression, and document-layout techniques. Segment documents into meaningful sections or chunks while preserving context, document identifiers, and source locations needed for subsequent processing and review.
  * Collaborate with subject-matter experts to define, document, implement, and test coding rules that transform extracted information into standardized data elements. Address exceptions, conflicting information, and ambiguous results through documented validation and review procedures.
  * Develop and maintain SQL and Oracle PL/SQL solutions for data transformation, validation, reconciliation, loading, and integration with operational databases and data warehouses.
