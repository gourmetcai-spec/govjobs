## What's a job series?

The federal government categorizes jobs into groups or families of jobs or occupations. These groups are called a series. The series is a four-digit number system for organizing similar jobs. Related jobs will have series numbers that are closer together.

For example, medical, dental and public health jobs will have a series between 0600-0699, where nurse jobs are in the 0610 series and nursing assistant is 0621.
