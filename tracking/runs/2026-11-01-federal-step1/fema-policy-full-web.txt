L133: ## Duties
L134:
L135: 【20†Help†usajobshelp.opm.gov】
L136: The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is calling on those who want to help protect American interests and secure our Nation. DHS Components work collectively to prevent terrorism; secure borders and our transportation systems; protect the President and other dignitaries; enforce and administer immigration laws; safeguard cyberspace; and ensure resilience to disasters. We achieve these vital missions through a workforce spanning hundreds of occupations. Make an impact; join DHS.
L137: When disaster strikes, America looks to the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA). Now FEMA looks to you. Join our team and use your talent to support Americans in their times of greatest need. FEMA prepares the nation for all hazards and manages Federal response and recovery efforts following any national incident. We foster innovation, reward performance and creativity, and provide challenges on a routine basis with a well-skilled, knowledgeable, high performance workforce.
L138: Please visit 【21†www.fema.gov†www.fema.gov】 for additional information.
L139: EXCEPTED SERVICE: This is a Schedule Policy/Career position in the excepted service.
L140: EMERGENCY ASSIGNMENT: Every FEMA employee has regular and recurring emergency management responsibilities, though not every position requires routine deployment to disaster sites. All positions are subject to recall around the clock for emergency management operations, which may require irregular work hours, work at locations other than the official duty station, and may include duties other than those specified in the employee's official position description.
L141: Travel requirements in support of emergency operations may be extensive in nature (weeks to months), with little advance notice, and may require employees to relocate to emergency sites with physically austere and operationally challenging conditions.
L142: In this position, you will serve as Chief, Financial Policy Branch within the Financial Management Division in FEMA's Office of the Chief Financial Officer. You will provide leadership, technical guidance and expertise on a wide range of financial and travel policies. . Typical assignments include:
L143:   * Directing staff to develop a mixture of routine and challenging financial and travel policy guidance based on organizational goals and objectives.
L144:   * Directing and overseeing daily work assignments, setting priorities, and determining resource requirements.
L145:   * Determining short- or long-term goals and strategies to achieve them; coordinating with other organizations to accomplish goals and objectives.
