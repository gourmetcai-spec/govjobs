Description

### Summary

This position is located in the Resilience Federal Insurance Directorate Insurance Acquisition & Technology Division. Ideal candidate for this position will be a automation specialist with experience researching emerging technologies and prototyping low-code tools, AI workflows, dashboards, and scripts to improve NFIP operations and services.

### Duties

What will I do in this position if hired? In this position, you will be an IT Specialist. Typical assignments include: Supporting the identification and analysis of opportunities for automation and AI enabled workflow improvement across NFIP business operations, customer facing services, map modernization support, underwriting, and other program areas in coordination with business SMEs, analysts, UX, engineering, and data stakeholders. Assisting in configuring, prototyping, and testing low code/no code solutions, AI agents, workflow automations, dashboards, forms, lightweight applications, and scripts (for example Python) or utilities to query, transform, create, or validate data and workflow outputs using approved enterprise platforms such as Power Platform, GCP enabled tools, DHSChat, Python libraries, or similar technologies. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Duties

Help

As an Operations Research Analyst, at the full performance you will perform the following duties:

  * Identified, formulated, and defined trends; designs research and study methods; developed, applied and interpreted appropriate models and methods in conducting operations research analysis; recommended new or amended legislation, policies and or procedures as they related to the effective and efficient management of the organizations resources.
  * Provide analytic concepts to provide validity of assumptions made and the criteria by which alternatives will be assessed.
  * Facilitates discussions with program-level operations research analyst(s) across the Agency including managers, subject matter experts, and law enforcement personnel to clarify issues and objectives to improve data quality and reporting.
  * Performs risk-related cost-benefit analyses; develops mitigation strategies to address those gaps/risks; offering recommendations to mitigate risks associated with not obtaining appropriated resources for staffing and budget.
  * Develops or recommends new policies and or procedures as they relate to the effective and efficient management of the organizations resources.
  * Facilitates meetings with program stakeholders thorough all levels of directorates.
  * Collaborates with Subject Matter Experts and Leadership to understand their program requirements. Clarifies issues into operational plans to understand their business process.
  * Provide support to Management and Programs for strategic planning and assistance related to data research, analysis, modeling design and development, reporting and communicating results.
