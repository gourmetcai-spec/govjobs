The primary focus of this role is conducting in-depth operational analyses, producing comprehensive reports, and ensuring rigorous data integrity to optimize the Smithsonian Institution’s Transportation Branch operations.

In this position, you will:

  * Serve as system administrator for the Transportation Branch FMIS. Oversee data governance to guarantee reporting accuracy and completeness. Compile FAST and federal submissions, managing system permissions, coordinating with OCIO, maintaining telematics/GPS/fuel interfaces, and designing dashboards/automated workflows.
  * Analyze operational goals, identifying systemic efficiency gaps, and evaluating program execution utilizing advanced analytical techniques. Integrate financial concepts into long-range fleet planning via cost-benefit, lease-vs-purchase, and lifecycle studies to guide capital planning and budget prioritization.
  * Identify administrative or operational issues and develop corrective actions. Participate in business requirement development, executing short- and long-range program adjustments, managing crosscutting initiatives, and performing liaison activities across multiple organizational and geographic divisions.
