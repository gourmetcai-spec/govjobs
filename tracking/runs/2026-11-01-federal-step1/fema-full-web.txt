L137: What will I do in this position if hired?
L138:
L139: In this position, you will be an IT Specialist. Typical assignments include:
L140:   * Supporting the identification and analysis of opportunities for automation and AI enabled workflow improvement across NFIP
L141: business operations, customer facing services, map modernization support, underwriting, and other program areas in
L142: coordination with business SMEs, analysts, UX, engineering, and data stakeholders.
L143:   * Assisting in configuring, prototyping, and testing low code/no code solutions, AI agents, workflow automations, dashboards, forms,
L144: lightweight applications, and scripts (for example Python) or utilities to query, transform, create, or validate data and workflow
L145: outputs using approved enterprise platforms such as Power Platform, GCP enabled tools, DHSChat, Python libraries, or similar
L146: technologies.
L147:   * Helping convert approved business and user requirements into working technical implementations by supporting workflow logic
L148: development, automation and AI agent configuration, data input and output definition, integration analysis, prototype
L149: documentation, and technical hand off materials for engineering, security, and operational teams.
L150:   * Conducting market, technology, and policy research on emerging automation, artificial intelligence, digital service, geospatial,
L151: insurance technology, and AI governance trends to help the Innovation Branch remain current and identify practical
L152: modernization opportunities.
L153:   * Supporting evaluation of prototypes and AI enabled workflows for feasibility, usability, accuracy, operational fit, and governance
L154: alignment through functional testing, user feedback, AI output review, exception testing, human in the loop validation,
L155: responsible AI practices, and coordination with engineering, cybersecurity, data, UX, geospatial, and business teams.
L156: What else do I need to know?
L157: At FEMA, our mission is to help people before, during and after disasters, and every employee at FEMA has a role in emergency management. Every FEMA employee has regular and recurring emergency management responsibilities, though not every position requires routine deployment to disaster sites.
L158: All positions are subject to recall around the clock for emergency management operations, which may require irregular work hours, work at locations other than the official duty station, and may include duties other than those specified in the employee's official position description.
L159: Travel requirements in support of emergency operations may be extensive in nature (weeks to months), with little advance notice, and may require employees to relocate to emergency sites with physically austere and operationally challenging conditions.
