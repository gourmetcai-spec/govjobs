Duties

 Help

 As a Financial Management Specialist, your typical work assignments may include:

Constructs quarterly continuing resolution models and restricted spending plans when full-year appropriations bills are delayed, establishing controlled spending levels that sustain mission-critical operations within the rate of operations authorized under the continuing resolution.

Oversees the formal distribution of approved appropriations to respective programs and sets funding templates in UFMS to control distributed funding levels and avoid overspending against allotted amounts.

Exercises delegated allowance approver authority at the highest level within the Administrative and Fiscal Unit, serving as the approving official for transactions that commit, obligate, and disburse SOD funds.

Identifies funding trends, execution risks, projected shortfalls, and resource requirements; and recommends corrective actions and funding adjustments to support mission objectives.

Qualifications

 For more information on eligibility requirements see: DEA Employment Eligibility

Applicants must meet all of the required qualification requirements described below by the closing of this announcement.

Applicants applying for the GS-13 must meet one of the following requirements:

A. Have at least one full year of qualifying specialized experience that is at least equivalent in difficulty and complexity to work performed at the GS-12 grade level and your experience includes performing at least three of the tasks below as part of your regular duties. Specialized experience refers to paid and unpaid experience, including volunteer work done through National Service programs (e.g., Peace Corps, AmeriCorps) and other organizations (e.g., professional; philanthropic; religious; spiritual; community, student, social). Volunteer work helps build critical competencies, knowledge, and skills and can provide valuable training and experience that translates directly to paid employment. You will receive credit for all qualifying experience, including volunteer experience.

Qualifying experience must demonstrate the following: 1) Developing operating budgets to meet programmatic and operational requirements; 2) Allocating approved appropriations and/or establishing funding controls in Unified Financial Management Systems (UFMS); 3) Approving financial transactions, evaluating discrepancies, and implementing corrective actions; and 4) Advising stakeholders on funding availability and fiscal requirements.

Additional information on the position qualification requirements are located in the Office of Personnel Management's (OPM) Qualifications Standards Handbook.

Time-In-Grade Requirement: Applicants must meet time-in-grade and time after competitive appointment requirements by the closing date of this announcement.

 Help

Please read the entire announcement and all the instructions before you begin an application. To apply and be considered for this position, you must complete all required questionnaires, assessments and submit all required documentation as specified in the How to Apply and Required Documents section. The complete application package must be submitted by 11:59 PM (ET) on the closing date of the announcement to receive consideration. The application process is as follows:

Click the Apply Online button.

Answer the questions presented in the application and attach all necessary supporting documentation.

Click the Submit Application button prior to 11:59 PM (ET) on the announcement closing date.

If you are required to complete any USA Hire Assessments, you will be notified after submitting your application. The notification will be provided in your application submission screen and via email. Your responses to the USA Hire Assessments will be reused for one year (in most cases) from the date you complete an assessment. If future applications you submit require completion of the same assessments, your responses will be automatically reused.

Visit the USA Hire Applicant Resource Center for practice assessments and assessment preparation resources at https://support-usahire.opm.gov/hc/en-us.

You may update your application documents and some questions related to your personal information at any time during the announcement open period. To make an allowed update to your application, return to your USAJOBS account (https://usajobs.gov). There you will find a record of your application, the application status, and an option to Edit my application. This option will no longer be available once the announcement has closed.

To view the announcement status or your application status: Visit https://www.usajobs.gov/Help/howto/application/status/.
