L26: ## Overview
L27:
L28: Full Stack Engineer 4 (AI Operations/MLX Group)
L29: Do you love building and pioneering in the technology space? Do you enjoy solving complex business problems in a fast-paced, collaborative, inclusive and iterative delivery environment? At Capital One, you'll be part of a big group of makers, breakers, doers and disruptors who solve real problems and meet real customer needs. We are seeking Full-stack Software Engineers who are passionate about leveraging cutting-edge open source frameworks and emerging technologies.
L30: In this role, you’ll be at the forefront of driving a major transformation across Capital One.
L31: What You’ll Do:
L32:   * Lead a portfolio of diverse technology projects and a team of developers with deep experience in distributed microservices, and full stack systems to create solutions that help meet regulatory needs for the company
L33:   * Share your passion for staying on top of tech trends, experimenting with and learning new technologies, participating in internal & external technology communities, mentoring other members of the engineering community
L34:   * Collaborate with digital product managers, and deliver robust cloud-based solutions that drive powerful experiences to help millions of Americans achieve financial empowerment
L35:   * Utilize programming languages like Java, Scala, Python, Go, Node.js, JavaScript and TypeScript, Open Source RDBMS and NoSQL databases, Container Orchestration services including Docker and Kubernetes, and a variety of AWS tools and services
L36:   * Provide technical leadership and direction across Agile teams, setting standards for design, development, and operational excellence
L37:   * Partner with architects to define service boundaries, integration patterns, and reusable frameworks
L38:   * Proactively identify areas for improvement, implementing targeted refactoring and contributing to efforts that reduce technical debt and enhance system performance
L39:   * Influence long-term technology strategy through evaluation of emerging tools and practices in a ‘you build it, you own it’ environment
L40: Basic Qualifications:
L41:
L42:   * Bachelor's Degree or higher in Computer Science or a related quantitative field (Statistics, Economics, Operations Research, Analytics, Mathematics, Engineering)
L43:   * At least 4 years of experience in software engineering (Internship experience does not apply)
L44:   * At least 2 years of experience with public cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure), cloud-based productivity tools, or virtualization technologies
L45:
L46: Preferred Qualifications:
L47:   * Master’s Degree in Computer Science or a related field
L48:   * 7+ years of experience coding with more than two of the following: Go, Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, Rust, C#, Scala
L49:   * 3+ years of experience with database and database management systems
L50:   * 3+ years of experience with AWS, GCP, Azure, or cloud-based productivity tools or virtualization technologies
L51:   * 4+ years of experience contributing to open source frameworks
L52:   * 3+ years of experience with front-end JavaScript or TypeScript frameworks
L53:   * 4+ years of experience creating synchronous and asynchronous APIs
L54:   * 3+ years of experience with CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code, and automated testing frameworks
L55:   * 3+ years of experience using Git-based version-control systems (e.g., Git, GitHub, Bitbucket)
L56:   * 3+ years of experience troubleshooting technical issues within cloud environments
L57:   * Experience leveraging modern AI-based coding tools and IDE copilots
L58:   * 1+ year of experience mentoring engineers and driving professional development within technical teams
L59:   * 1+ year of experience leveraging observability to identify and optimize performance bottlenecks in distributed systems
L60:   * Experience with interactive AI tooling to accelerate productivity, utilizing capabilities beyond basic code completion
L61:   * Experience leading cross-functional engineering efforts or large-scale technical initiatives
L62:   * Experience establishing and enforcing coding standards and design patterns across teams
