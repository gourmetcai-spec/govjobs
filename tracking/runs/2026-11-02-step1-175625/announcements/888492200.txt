Duties

 Help

Duty 1: Responsible for the design, development, installation, test, implementation, modification, documentation and follow-up maintenance of systems software for the Kessel Run Experimental Lab (KREL). Performs in-depth analysis.

Duty 2: Serves as expert planner, analyst and advisor for data management. Serves as technical advisor to management, responsible for evaluating and providing recommendations of new database technologies and architectures and/or developing and administrating data standards, policies, and procedures.

Duty 3: Participates as an expert on software, hardware, communications and new technology. Performs feasibility studies which involve evaluating proposals regarding modification or replacement of current software or hardware and development or acquisition of new technology.

Duty 4: As an IT expert, provides consultation and instruction. Advises functional area users on database and file accessing

 techniques, search strategies, processing and space utilization efficiencies, database security procedures, backup and program

 recovery techniques.

Duty 5: Judiciously manages the acquisition, development and implementation of new technology. Manages all aspects of new

 or modified information systems, subsystems or equipment items to meet cost, schedule, performance and supportability

 criteria.

KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES (KSAs): Your qualifications will be evaluated on the basis of your level of knowledge, skills, abilities and/or competencies in the following areas:

1. Mastery of and skill in applying a wide range of software and data analysis concepts and techniques, IT application software concepts, design principles, methods, practices and approaches.

2. Extensive knowledge of operating system theories and concepts, the interrelationships of multiple IT disciplines and the operating environment.

3. Extensive knowledge of database management systems, operating systems, technical architecture and network topology sufficient to diagnose and resolve the most complex database management problems and issues.

4. Broad knowledge of information technology processing standards and policies and familiarity with acquisition procedures sufficient to advise program manager(s) of information technology projects.

5. Broad knowledge of IT security principles and methods sufficient to recognize violations.

6. Knowledge of computer requirements and techniques in carrying out project assignments consisting of several tasks simultaneously; as well as documenting technical methods and procedures, to sufficiently lead design teams required to develop products to meet customers' needs.

Volunteer work helps build critical competencies, knowledge and skills that can provide valuable training and experience that translates directly to paid employment.You will receive credit for all qualifying experience, including volunteer experience.
