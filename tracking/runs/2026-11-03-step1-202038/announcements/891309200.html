Office of General Counsel

The Office of General Counsel (OGC) provides legal advice and representation to every office in FHFA regarding all aspects of FHFA's activities and operations.

Position Overview

The Senior Program Analyst (Oversight) will serve as the operational engine that supports oversight, audit, congressional affairs, and other Agency staff and ensure that Agency processes, documentation, task management, and cross-office communication related to oversight inquiries are accurate, timely, well-organized, and compliant with Agency policy. This position will also provide operational services and support for Congressional relations as directed by the OCAC, assisting in tasks necessary to prepare the Agency for Congressional inquiries, briefings, and productions, but without serving as the coordinator of Congressional relations.

Major Duties & Responsibilities

  • Serve as the principal operational coordinator for all external audits, oversight engagements, and Requests for Information (RFIs), ensuring alignment with the Agency's Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and legal guidance
  • Conduct analysis of processes and activities undertaken and work to develop creative solutions to highly problematic program areas identified.
  • Support and, when appropriate, represent the Agency in Congressional briefings, hearings, transmittals, and inquiries in coordination with the Office of Congressional Affairs and Communications while providing operational support services for Congressional relations as directed by the OCAC, including preparing materials for inquiries, transmittals, briefings, hearings, and staff requests (non-legal content).
  • Manage centralized tracking of incoming Congressional inquiries, deadlines, responses, supporting documentation, and production schedules, following workflow guidance from the OCAC.
  • Serve as the operational hub linking OGC (Litigation Branch), OCAC, and the Internal Controls and Compliance Branch ensuring unified, consistent, and disciplined oversight workflows across all phases of an audit or Congressional response.
  • Review administrative reports processes, identify areas for improvement, develop and implement enhanced changes or corrective action(s) needed.
  • Develop, refine, and maintain tracking systems, dashboards, SOPs, and production workflows to strengthen audit readiness and Congressional response operations.
  • Lead comprehensive reviews of key program policies to find gaps or determine needed updates; develop new or modified policies, issuances, and directives as appropriate; and serve as the key catalyst in the adoption and implementation of policy proposals developed.
  • Serve as a high-level consultant and authoritative advisor to executive management and external stakeholders; deliver decision-ready briefings, policy/program recommendations, and strategic reports to senior management.
  • Advise management on the effectiveness and efficiency of oversight activities and initiatives; identify areas for improvement and recommend creative solutions to address areas of concern.
  • Perform other duties as assigned.

Qualifications

You may qualify for your desired series and grade level if you meet the following qualification requirements:

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS:

Minimum Qualification EL-15: Applicants must have 52 weeks (one-year) of specialized experience equivalent to the next lower grade level as described below.

Experience: I qualify for the EL/GS-15 because I have at least 52 weeks of specialized experience at the EL/GS-14 or equivalent demonstrating work experience with the statements described below.

Examples of specialized experience include:

  • Managing or handling Congressional inquiries, investigations, hearings, or oversight engagements either on behalf of a federal agency/organization or on the part of congresspeople or committees.
  • Coordinating responses or deliverables across an agency/organization to ensure unified, accurate, consistent, and timely work products while driving efficiencies in processes and workflows.
  • Interpreting policies, guidance, or program requirements to develop goals, target timelines, and performance metrics, and monitoring progress towards meeting those goals.
  • Translating operational requirements, status information, and risks into clear, decision-ready materials for senior leadership, program offices, and other key stakeholders.

Selective Placement Factor: This is an additional minimum requirement to determine if you have the experience necessary for the position.
  • Must have demonstrated extensive experience managing and coordinating interactions with congressional committees related to banking, financial services, housing, or oversight on behalf of a federal agency/organization or on the part of congresspeople.

Time-In-Grade: Current or former Federal government employees who have held an EL/GS position in the preceding 52 weeks, must meet the time-in-grade requirement.
  • To be eligible for the EL-15 position, applicants must have served 52 weeks as an EL/GS-14 or higher in the Federal Service.

Experience refers to paid and unpaid experience, including volunteer work done through National Service programs (e.g., Peace Corps, AmeriCorps) and other organizations (e.g., professional; philanthropic; religious; spiritual; community, student, social). Volunteer work helps build critical competencies, knowledge, and skills and can provide valuable training and experience that translates directly to paid employment. You will receive credit for all qualifying experience, including volunteer experience.

In addition, certain FHFA employees are prohibited from accepting compensation from Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac for a two-year period after terminating employment with FHFA. Furthermore, to avoid financial conflicts-of interest or the appearance of conflicts-of-interest, FHFA employees may need to divest or sell certain assets they, their spouse, or minor children own or control, including securities issued by Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, or the Federal Home Loan Banks. Employees who work on Federal Home Loan Bank issues may need to sell or divest financial interests with any of the Federal Home Loan Bank members, which may include stock in bank holding companies, insurance companies, and other financial services firms. Questions regarding these requirements and prohibitions should be directed to the Office of General Counsel at (202) 649-3088.
