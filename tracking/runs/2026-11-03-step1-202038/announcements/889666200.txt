Duties

 Help

 The successful candidate will perform the full range of Senior Financial Analyst (TL) duties.

Such duties include but are not limited to:

Serves as a senior financial management authority within FCAB, leading agency-wide budget execution and oversee NRC resources.

Directs budget activities across Prior Year, Budget Year, and Budget Year+1 cycles to support agency programs and priorities.

Oversees financial controls, commitments and obligations, accounting string validation, and funding realignments to ensure accurate and compliant financial operations.

Applies data analytics and business intelligence tools to enhance financial reporting, support decision-making, and improve operational effectiveness.

Advises senior leadership on budget execution, resource allocation, and financial management strategies.

Communicates complex financial information and collaborates with stakeholders across the agency to support mission objectives.

Qualifications

 In order to qualify for this position, you must have at least one year of specialized experience at the next lower grade level (GG-13) in the Federal service or equivalent experience in the private or public sector.

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE is defined as experience leading agency-wide budget execution, funds control, and financial management operations across multiple fiscal year cycles. This experience must include applying data analytics, financial modeling, business intelligence tools (such as Power BI, Tableau, or advanced spreadsheet applications) to analyze complex financial data, monitor commitments and obligations, and provide strategic insights to executive leadership.

The ideal candidate will be able to demonstrate the following:

Knowledge and experience with Federal budget execution principles, fiscal law, and agencywide funds control

Ability to research, analyze, and interpret data from financial reports to identify anomalies, evaluate fiscal issues, and draw accurate conclusions

Experience utilizing data analytics, business intelligence tools, and advanced financial systems to drive strategic insights and decision-making

Strong administrative, leadership, and team coordination skills sufficient to direct a team, manage priorities, and foster continuous learning.

Advanced communication and interpersonal skills to present complex financial information clearly and collaborate effectively across all organizational levels.

PLEASE BE CLEAR AND CONCISE. WE WILL NOT MAKE ASSUMPTIONS REGARDING YOUR EXPERIENCE.

 Nuclear Regulatory Commission

 If you are curious, inspired, or interested in bringing your energy to a committed team in a dynamic science, engineering, technical, or administrative career, consider the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC). We are professionals dedicated to protecting people and the environment by safeguarding the use of nuclear materials in medicine, industry, and research.

NRC is an independent agency established to protect public health and safety as nuclear energy fuels electrical power and a variety of industrial, medical, and research applications. We come from varied educational and experiential backgrounds-the military, industry, and academia.

The NRC prides itself on creating a work environment rich in opportunity, leadership, training, teamwork and work life balance. Help guide our nation into the next generation of nuclear safety by completing your USAJOBs profile and reviewing our job announcements! Begin a challenging career with the NRC where you can be a part of a select group of professionals who protect people and the environment with the peaceful use of nuclear materials in medicine, industry and research.
