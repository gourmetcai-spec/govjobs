Duties

 Help

As an IT Specialist (Network Services), you will perform the following duties:

Initiate engineering research, architecture design, identification, procurement, and deployment of Information Technology (IT)-related hardware, software, high-capacity transport circuits, and cloud connectivity service at the enterprise level. Structure strategic direction, technical governance, and contract oversight for N-Wave contract personnel, commercial telecommunications carriers, and Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) to streamline end-to-end service delivery across N-Wave infrastructure and hybrid cloud offerings.

Support executive-level architecture and design reviews with N-Wave engineering leads, cloud teams, and senior line office stakeholders to establish technical approaches for complex service requests. Share communication with executive National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) leadership across Line Offices. Synthesize complex networking, optical transport, and cloud-edge realities into strategic roadmaps, briefings, and decision memos in support of NOAA Chief Information Officer enterprise goals.

Show skill in applying, enterprise-level IT and network infrastructure. Structure projects using program and portfolio management frameworks, including Waterfall, Hybrid, and Agile methodologies. Standards for enterprise network and cybersecurity compliance are applied. Show advanced technical knowledge and broad operational experience in Wide Area Networking (WAN), high-capacity optical transport, and direct-connect cloud connectivity.

POSITION REQUIREMENTS:

This is a Critical sensitive, ADP position. A Top Secret security clearance is required.

Throughout the recruitment and hiring process we will be communicating with you via email; therefore, it is imperative that the email address you provide when applying for this vacancy remains active. Should your email address change, please notify the point of contact identified in the vacancy announcement as soon as possible so that we can update our system.

EDUCATION: There is no positive education requirement for this position.

SPECIALIZED EXPERIENCE: Applicants must possess one year of specialized experience equivalent in difficulty and responsibility to the next lower grade level in the Federal Service. Specialized experience is experience that has equipped the applicant with the particular competencies/knowledge, skills and abilities to successfully perform the duties of the position. This experience need not have been in the federal government.

Experience refers to paid and unpaid experience, including volunteer work done through National Service programs (e.g., Peace Corps, AmeriCorps) and other organizations e.g., professional; philanthropic, religious; spiritual; community, student, social). Volunteer work helps build critical competencies; knowledge, and skills and can provide valuable training and experience that translates directly to paid employment. You will receive credit for all qualifying experience, including volunteer experience.

Oral Communication - Expresses information (for example, ideas or facts) to individuals or groups effectively, taking into account the audience and nature of the information (for example, technical, sensitive, controversial); makes clear and convincing oral presentations; listens to others, attends to nonverbal cues, and responds appropriately.

Problem Solving - Identifies problems; determines accuracy and relevance of information; uses sound judgment to generate and evaluate alternatives, and to make recommendations.

How you will be evaluated

You will be evaluated for this job based on how well you meet the qualifications above.

You will be evaluated based on how well you meet the qualifications listed in this vacancy announcement. Your qualifications will be evaluated based on your application materials (e.g., resume, supporting documents), your responses on the application questionnaire and/or evaluation through a manual assessment process, and your responses to any additional assessments (i.e. USAHire) required for this position.

Your qualifications will be also be evaluated using a Job Knowledge Test. The Job Knowledge Test is designed to assess technical and professional expertise directly relevant to the requirements of this announcement. This assessment measures the specialized knowledge and skills accumulated over time, which are critical for successful performance in this position. The best-qualified candidates will be identified for referral to the hiring manager and may be invited for an interview.

CATEGORY RATING:

Category rating will be used in the ranking and selection process for this position. Within each category, those entitled to Veterans' preference will be listed at the top of the pre-defined category for which they are placed. The quality categories are Gold, Silver, and Bronze. Preference eligible with a service-connected disability of 10% or more will be listed at the top of the highest quality category (Gold) depending on the position and grade level of the job. For more information on Category Rating, please go to Category Rating.

Your responses to the application questions and/or assessment method will determine your placement in the appropriate quality category described above. Your responses must be substantiated by your resume and supporting documents. If you do not respond to the application questions you may be rated ineligible. Candidates placed in the "Gold" category will be identified first for referral to the hiring manager and may be invited for an interview.
