Duties

Help
  • Directs the preparation, analysis, and execution of the NAF annual operating budget, APF/NAF support budget, Capital Purchases and Minor Construction (CPMC) and major NAF/APF construction budgets, NAF procurement functions, Five Year Plan, etc.
  • Conducts cost and economic analysis, efficiency studies, and organization functional analysis. Formulates and maintains a system of internal management controls. Establishes policies, plans, procedures, reviews and audits.
  • Exercises financial oversight for installation private organizations, and certifies availability of funds for expenditures.
  • Uses automation software and hardware to perform analytical, technical, and administrative tasks. Insures APF and NAF operating fiscal budgets are fully synchronized and balanced according to fund sources.
  • Executes, monitors, coordinates, and provides oversight to the operating and reimbursable budget programs. Gathers, verifies and consolidates a variety of budget/financial data to be used in the formulation of spreadsheets and presentations.
  • Researches, verifies, and revises budget/program data. Prepares funding justifications, consolidates budget requests, requests additional funds, and monitors expenditures. Evaluates financial statements and prepares reports and presentations.
  • Performs all of the duties normally associated with supervising staff.

Qualifications

Resumes must be two pages (or less). Resumes should clearly demonstrate the applicant's relevant experience, skills, knowledge and abilities as they pertain to this position.

A qualified candidate must possess the following:

  • Ability to perform the duties described above using the theories, principles, practices,and techniques of generally accepted accounting principles.
  • Work experience interpreting and assessing program financial performance using standard financial management systems.
  • Work experience interpreting balance sheet and profit and loss statement results.
  • Work experience applying commercial budgeting techniques.
  • Supervisory experience directing technical and professional financial management operations.
  • Progressively responsible work experience in budget or finance that provided the incumbent the knowledge of APF/NAF regulations on budgetary processes and requirements.
  • Knowledge and ability to balance funds between accounts; i.e. transferring funds between line items or sub-projects or identifying excess funds for possible transfer to other operating programs.
  • Must have excellent organizational skills.
  • Must be highly skilled in written and verbal communications and able to deal tactfully and effectively with employees in all levels of the organization.
  • Must be proficient in Microsoft Windows, Word, Excel, and other software as required.
  • Must be able to meet stringent deadlines in a fast-paced environment.

In addition to the above qualifications, candidates who possess all of the following qualifications will be considered "Highly Preferred" or "Best Qualified":
  1. Work experience of at least three (3) years with NAF budget planning and execution. (Ability to apply financial accounting principles, methods and techniques of fiscal management, with a strong background in budget preparation, payroll management, internal controls and financial systems reporting.)
  2. Possess experience coordinating manpower and staffing issues with NAF personnel office and Epay systems.
  3. Experience analyzing monthly NAF financial reports to include budget variance, Major Construction/Restoration, CPMC execution and cash flow projections.
  4. Possess financial management experience necessary to execute an effective acquisition and financial management plan to include demonstrated ability to determine resource needs and allocation of resources for both appropriate funds (APF) and non-appropriated funds (NAF) budgets and analysis of financial data.
***Applicants who meet the minimum qualifications and "Highly Preferred Criteria" will be referred to the selecting official for consideration first. All other applicants may be referred if requested by the selecting official. ***

Help

Working for Army Non-Appropriated Funds (NAF) can offer numerous advantages. Army NAF employment can provide a sense of pride and fulfillment, as you'll be supporting communities comprised of military and civilian personnel, and their families, which contributes to the Department of Defense's overall mission. We offer a wide range of career opportunities, including jobs in childcare, recreation, hospitality, and more. Many employees have opportunities for professional development, including training, education, and certification programs to help towards career advancement. As an Army NAF employee, you'll have access to some on-base facilities, including fitness centers, libraries, and many other amenities.

Regular full-time and part-time employees are eligible for enrollment in health and life insurance, participation in 401k and retirement plans, and paid personal time off and federal holidays. NAF flexible employees, working in the United States or a U.S. citizen or spouse or child of a U.S. citizen working for a NAFI in a foreign area, are eligible to participate in the NAF 401(k) plan. Some flexible employees may also be eligible to receive health insurance.
