Duties

 Help

You will perform financial management, accounting, and audit duties by reviewing budgets, examining financial records, verifying transactions, and ensuring funds are documented, justified, controlled, and used according to laws and policies.

You will work with program staff to prepare budget estimates, manage annual funding allocations, create spending plans, and schedule acquisitions while ensuring resources are distributed efficiently and support established program requirements.

You will study financial data, assess management processes, and recommend improvements to reporting methods, financial guidance, and internal controls while developing procedures that improve accuracy, consistency, accountability, and compliance.

You will interpret financial policies, executive orders, official memoranda, and budget guidance to recommend solutions, resolve complex financial issues, explain requirements, and establish consistent procedures across organizational programs.

You will monitor approved budgets and review financial reports to track funding allocations, commitments, and expenditures, determine when funds should be released, and identify shortfalls or inconsistencies that require management attention.

You will prepare financial reports, briefings, schedules, and recommendations using data from official and unofficial systems to evaluate program progress, measure results, identify risks, and support funding and resource allocation decisions.

You will advise managers on procurement activities, acquisition planning, and the use of appropriated funds while ensuring compliance with financial requirements and supporting research, development, testing, evaluation, and maintenance programs.

Additional information

The Marine Corps Financial Management Specialist executes acquisition efforts across the FDE Enterprise, PAE Marine Corps, and PAE Mission Systems.

The financial manager serves as an Integrated Product Team (IPT) subject matter expert, advising on the integration of financial regulations into acquisition planning, budget formulation, and execution processes. In this role, they independently prepare, justify, and defend budget exhibits, manage the flow of program funds while ensuring strict compliance with fiscal law to prevent violations, and conduct advanced financial, predictive, and trend analyses while ensuring financial audit compliance.
