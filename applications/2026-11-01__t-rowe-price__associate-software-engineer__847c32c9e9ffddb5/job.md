Role Summary
Join T. Rowe Price's Global Product Technology team as an Associate Software Engineer to help build the next generation of product data platforms and client experiences. This entry-level role is ideal for early-career software engineers looking to grow their technical skills while working on modern cloud-based applications. You'll collaborate with experienced engineers and business partners to design, develop, test, and support scalable solutions using technologies such as microservices, cloud computing, distributed databases, and AI. Working in an Agile environment, you'll contribute across the full software development lifecycle while gaining hands-on experience solving real-world business and technical challenges.

Responsibilities
Contributes to all parts of the SDLC, e.g., design, development, and testing
Develops technical solutions following standard methodologies with some guidance from senior engineers
Identifies and remediates technical debt
Works with existing data models and schemas and suggests changes and improvements where necessary
Demonstrates strong logic and reasoning capabilities
Delivers on well-specified work items
Uses questions and proposals to clarify requirements when unclear

Qualifications
Required:
BS or MS Degree in a technical discipline and 1+ years of demonstrated hands-on software engineering experience.  Additional experience may substitute for degree
Knowledge of SQL, Relational and Analytical Database Concepts and Stored Procedures
Strong Test-Driven Development and desire to write simple, adaptive and iterative code
Experience working in a dynamic, fast-paced, Agile team environment
Enthusiasm for learning & results oriented
Debugs large components with limited assistance and assists other engineers
Designs and develops practical APIs and abstractions
Able to operate with openness and efficiency with one or more SDLC methodologies (e.g., Scrum)
Actively seeks feedback and guidance to improve technical skills (e.g., through submitting work for code review)
Demonstrates competence in one or two programming languages or technologies
Works with existing data models and schemas and suggests changes and improvements where necessary
Debugs and fixes your own software with minimal assistance

Preferred:
Experience building applications and deploying to public or private clouds, such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, or similar providers
Understanding of AI-driven tools, workflow automation, or analytics-enabled business solutions.
Financial industry experience
