## Summary

The Office of the Inspector General (OIG) is one of the best places to work in the Department of Justice and is one of the premier inspector general offices in federal government. The OIG's Audit Division is seeking highly motivated technology professionals to support the agency's mission to promote integrity, efficiency, and accountability within the Department of Justice. The incumbent will serve as an Applications Engineer in the Office of Management Services.

## Duties

As a GS-13 IT Development and Analysis (Enterprise Applications Engineer), your responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:
Managing and modifying new or existing enterprise applications in Power Platform or equivalent platforms.
Maintaining databases, schema, and views used by reporting tools.
Managing database management systems, including data entry, retrieval, and maintenance.
Administering day-to-day enterprise applications to facilitate organizational project management while ensuring attention to details related to compliance, user needs, and mission objectives.
Integrating reporting components from multiple data sources.
Analyzing and documenting existing data, data flows, data requirements, and reporting needs.
Quickly responding to customer needs by adjusting reports that meet and exceed customer expectations.
Communicating information to technical and non-technical audiences.
Anticipating, identifying, and resolving issues during the change design phase to prevent problems in the development and deployment stages, thereby avoiding costly rework.
Assessing complex data scenarios and choosing strategies that provide solutions aligned with business objectives.
Demonstrating strong interpersonal skills to build and maintain relationships with stakeholders, facilitating clear communication and understanding of reporting and analytics needs.
Collaborating effectively within a multidisciplinary team environment to ensure seamless integration of enterprise-wide solutions and cross-functional alignment.
Contributing to solution design documentation, such as governance, architecture, data management, and access control documents, including presenting this information to end users and other stakeholders.
Responsibilities will increase and assignments will become more complex as your training and experience progress.
