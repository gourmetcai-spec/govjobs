## Summary

This position is located in the Resilience Federal Insurance Directorate Insurance Acquisition & Technology Division. Ideal candidate for this position will be a automation specialist with experience researching emerging technologies and prototyping low-code tools, AI workflows, dashboards, and scripts to improve NFIP operations and services.

## Duties

What will I do in this position if hired?
In this position, you will be an IT Specialist. Typical assignments include:
Supporting the identification and analysis of opportunities for automation and AI enabled workflow improvement across NFIP
	business operations, customer facing services, map modernization support, underwriting, and other program areas in
	coordination with business SMEs, analysts, UX, engineering, and data stakeholders.
Assisting in configuring, prototyping, and testing low code/no code solutions, AI agents, workflow automations, dashboards, forms,
	lightweight applications, and scripts (for example Python) or utilities to query, transform, create, or validate data and workflow
	outputs using approved enterprise platforms such as Power Platform, GCP enabled tools, DHSChat, Python libraries, or similar
	technologies.
Helping convert approved business and user requirements into working technical implementations by supporting workflow logic
	development, automation and AI agent configuration, data input and output definition, integration analysis, prototype
	documentation, and technical hand off materials for engineering, security, and operational teams.
Conducting market, technology, and policy research on emerging automation, artificial intelligence, digital service, geospatial,
	insurance technology, and AI governance trends to help the Innovation Branch remain current and identify practical
	modernization opportunities.
Supporting evaluation of prototypes and AI enabled workflows for feasibility, usability, accuracy, operational fit, and governance
	alignment through functional testing, user feedback, AI output review, exception testing, human in the loop validation,
	responsible AI practices, and coordination with engineering, cybersecurity, data, UX, geospatial, and business teams.
What else do I need to know?
At FEMA, our mission is to help people before, during and after disasters, and every employee at FEMA has a role in emergency management. Every FEMA employee has regular and recurring emergency management responsibilities, though not every position requires routine deployment to disaster sites. All positions are subject to recall around the clock for emergency management operations, which may require irregular work hours, work at locations other than the official duty station, and may include duties other than those specified in the employee's official position description. Travel requirements in support of emergency operations may be extensive in nature (weeks to months), with little advance notice, and may require employees to relocate to emergency sites with physically austere and operationally challenging conditions.
