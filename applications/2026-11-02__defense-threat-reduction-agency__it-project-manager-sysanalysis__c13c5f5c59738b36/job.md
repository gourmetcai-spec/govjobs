## Summary

This position may be filled using Direct Hire Authority: Z5CAV/Direct-Hire Authority (Certain DoD Personnel) PL 118-31, Sec 125OB (i)(2), 12/22/2023. This position is being filled under the memorandum from the Under Secretary of Defense for Personnel and Readiness (USD(P&R)) "Expansion of Direct Hire Authority for Certain Personnel of the Department of Defense," dated August 12,2024.
This position is part of the Defense Threat Reduction Agency.

## Duties

As a IT PROJECT MANAGER (SYSANALYSIS) at the GS-2210-12/13 some of your typical work assignments may include:
Lead and manage the entire lifecycle of IT software development projects, ensuring solutions are delivered on time, within budget, and in compliance with DTRA and DoD guidelines.
Serve as the primary liaison between stakeholders, technical teams, and direct (GS-15/O-6), and senior (SES/FO) leadership, translating complex requirements and communicating project status, risks, and strategic alignment.
Direct the requirements lifecycle by eliciting, analyzing, and translating complex operational needs into clear, testable user stories for Agile/DevSecOps development teams.
Provide high-level technical advice and strategic guidance for the DevSecOps portfolio, shaping the technical vision, architectural standards, and innovation roadmap.
Oversee the design, construction, and maintenance of secure, automated CI/CD pipelines, and manage the enterprise software development toolchain to enable continuous delivery.
Deliver technical quality control for all projects by reviewing and approving technical specifications, enforcing security standards, and resolving system integration challenges.

## Qualifications

You may qualify at the GS-12, if you fulfill the following qualifications:
A. One year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-11 grade level in the Federal service as listed below:
Supported the deployment of secure software pipelines and containerized applications, utilizing automated configurations and following established technical guidelines.
Collaborated within cross-functional teams using Agile and DevSecOps principles to assist in delivering secure, continuous software solutions.
Assisted as a technical resource and liaison between teams, helping to translate operational requirements into clear, testable development tasks and user stories.
Developed project cost estimates and prepared progress briefings for program managers to support project planning and leadership decision-making.
You may qualify at the GS-13 if you fulfill the following qualifications:
A. One year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-12 grade level in the Federal service as listed below:
Led software development projects from initial planning through final delivery, ensuring all products met strict organizational security and quality standards.
Guided diverse technical teams using modern Agile and security practices to establish continuous, automated software testing and delivery pipelines.
Served as a key technical advisor to senior leadership, shaping long-term technology roadmaps and translating complex technical needs into actionable development plans.
Managed secure software development tools and automated cloud deployments, including containerized applications and detailed project cost estimations.
Experience refers to paid and unpaid experience, including volunteer work done through National Service programs (e.g., Peace Corps, AmeriCorps) and other organizations (e.g., professional; philanthropic; religious; spiritual; community, student, social). Volunteer work helps build critical competencies, knowledge, and skills and can provide valuable training and experience that translates directly to paid employment. You will receive credit for all qualifying experience, including volunteer experience.
In addition to meeting qualifications, your application package must reflect the applicable experience to meet the Individual Occupational Requirements for the 2210, series as listed below:
Experience must be IT related; the experience may be demonstrated by paid or unpaid experience and/or completion of specific, intensive training (for example, IT certification), as appropriate GS-5 through GS-15 (or equivalent): For all positions individuals must have IT-related experience demonstrating each of the four competencies listed below.
