Senior Associate Director - Financial Stability
Summary
Reporting to the division director and in coordination with other senior officers, the senior associate director oversees the economic research function(s) of the division. This position provides senior level oversight and support to officers and section chiefs in managing resources to address the ongoing needs of the division and assures high-quality resolutions of responsibilities and critical work demands.
This position is located in Washington, DC.
Principal Duties and Responsibilities
Serves as a key member of the division’s senior leadership team; models and fosters a collaborative culture in the division, the Board, and the Federal Reserve System. Builds effective and productive relationships with stakeholders and leverages their resources to execute on the financial stability strategy.
Oversees the research and policy analysis of one or more of the division’s research sections.
Provides intellectual leadership and guidance on the coverage, research, and analysis of emerging and recurring financial stability issues.
Contributes to the development of high-quality, policy-relevant briefing materials, memos, and background materials for the Board and the FOMC, both as an individual contributor and as part of a team.  Considers ways to innovate on existing materials to ensure they are as effective as possible.
Participates in the division’s research activities, workshops, and seminars; helps foster a productive and rewarding research environment; actively seeks to bring new insights from academic research to the attention of Board policymakers and staff, particularly on policy relevant topics; facilitates ongoing interactions between the Board and the research community; serves as a role model through ongoing direct engagement in research activities.
Actively coaches and mentors division staff in furthering their research, including providing advice and guidance on research projects, writing papers, and navigating the publication process.
Contributes to the activities of the division and Board that promote the division’s values and priorities, foster a culture of collaboration and collegiality, and enhance job performance and job satisfaction for staff.
Develops, manages, and strengthens relationships that establish trust and confidence with domestic regulators, foreign finance ministries, other central banks, other official sector contacts, Bank for International Settlements, G-groups, International Monetary Fund, World Bank, and Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development, working with other staff at the Board, the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, and the U.S. Treasury, where applicable.
Provides intellectual leadership and guidance on the coverage, research, and analysis of emerging and recurring financial stability issues.
Promotes research, information sharing, and collaboration among staff and others across the divisions. Actively engages with senior level officers in FS division and other divisions at the Board. Oversees joint projects as requested. Maintains strong working relationships with these groups by leading and providing strategic direction of the division and the achievement of its objectives and priorities.
May be assigned additional duties not listed.
Minimum Qualifications: To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill, and/or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.
