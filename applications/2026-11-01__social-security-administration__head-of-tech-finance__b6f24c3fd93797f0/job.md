L133: ## Duties
L134:
L135: 【19†Help†usajobshelp.opm.gov】
L136:
L137: The Head of Tech Finance is accountable for the integration of financial planning, resource allocation, and compliance for IT applications, projects, and operations, ensuring alignment with SSA's mission, Federal priorities, and statutory requirements. The Head:
L138:   * Provides executive leadership and strategic direction for enterprise-wide IT financial management, partnering with the Chief Financial Officer, Chief Information Officer, and Strategic Sourcing leadership to develop and execute IT financial strategies, policies, and programs aligned with SSA's mission and Federal priorities
L139:   * Oversees the formulation, justification, and execution of multi-year and annual IT budgets, including Capital Planning and Investment Control (CPIC) processes, cost-benefit analyses, risk assessments, and investment reviews to optimize resource utilization and advance agency modernization goals.
L140:   * Directs the allocation, execution, and monitoring of IT financial resources to ensure effective stewardship of public funds, robust internal controls, audit readiness, and compliance with Federal laws, Office of Management and Budget (OMB) guidance, and statutory requirements.
L141:   * Develops and implements agency-wide IT financial management policies, procedures, and standards, ensuring alignment with the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), Federal Information Technology Acquisition Reform Act (FITARA), OMB Circular A-11, and other applicable Federal statutes.
L142:   * Serves as principal advisor to senior leadership on IT financial implications, representing SSA in engagements with OMB, Congress, and interagency partners, and providing authoritative guidance on legislative, policy, and emerging technology issues affecting IT financial planning.

L179: ECQ 5: Achieving Results - Demonstrated ability to achieve both individual and organizational results, and to align results to stated goals from superiors.

L334: ## Social Security Administration
L335: The men and women of the Social Security Administration are the stewards of the Federal Retirement, Survivors and Disability Insurance programs, as well as Supplemental Security Income for the aged, blind and disabled. With a workforce of approximately 50,000 employees in 1,300 service locations across the country, our mission is to advance the economic security of the nation's people through compassionate and vigilant leadership in shaping and managing America's Social Security programs.
