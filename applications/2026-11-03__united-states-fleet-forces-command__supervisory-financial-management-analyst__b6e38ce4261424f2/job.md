## Qualifications

Your resume must demonstrate at least one year of specialized experience at or equivalent to the GS-13 grade level or pay band in the Federal service or equivalent experience in the private or public sector. Specialized experience must demonstrate the following:
1. Experience in applying Federal financial management laws, regulations, and guidance from GAO, OMB, Treasury, and DoW; serves as a subject-matter authority and advisor on the interpretation and application of financial policy
2. Experience and knowledge of DOW/DON financial management regulations, enterprise business systems, appropriations law, and audit requirements sufficient to develop new or modified financial management policies, procedures, and internal control frameworks.
3. Experience with Federal budget formulation, execution, accounting, and reporting; skilled at assessing enterprise financial risk, evaluating long-term strategic fiscal impacts, and designing innovative mitigation strategies.
4. Experience with complex financial studies across budget and accounting systems. Skilled in resolving issues with incomplete data/evolving rules for massive, high-profile programs of national significance & Congressional interest.
5. Experience and expert ability to extract, integrate, and aggregate large volumes of financial and programmatic data from multiple enterprise systems to identify systemic weaknesses, emerging risks, and cross-functional impacts.
6. Experience and knowledge of organizational missions, strategic objectives, and funding architectures sufficient to align financial policy and execution with enterprise priorities.
7. Ability to advise senior leaders on high-impact financial matters. Exercise authoritative judgment where guidance is vague, conflicting, or silent, making precedent-setting decisions for major programs.

Duties

 Help

You will serve as the Deputy Comptroller and Deputy Director of Financial Management for COMSUBLANT.

You will serve as senior advisor to the Commander, Executive Director, Chief of Staff and senior leadership on all fiscal, budgetary, and resource management matters, with a significant focus on providing strategic financial oversight and guidance.

You will be responsible for the overall development, formulation, justification, and execution of the Submarine Force operating budget, which includes both long and short-range financial plans for direct and reimbursable funding.

You will exercise command-wide authority and accountability for establishing financial policy, strategic resource planning, programming, budgeting, accounting, and execution of all financial operations.

You will be responsible for providing worldwide support and for deployed ports supporting over 12,000 military and civilian personnel and managing an annual operating budget of approximately $400 million

You will be responsible for $4.5 million in reimbursable programs and required to frequently collaborate and provide financial guidance/advice to COMSUBPAC Comptroller and Deputy Comptroller.

You will be responsible for ensuring integration of all fiscal operations in accordance with Federal laws, DoD/DON directives, audit readiness standards FIAR/Audit, Navy Enterprise Resource Planning (NERP), and evolving best practices.
