Job Description

THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE
The Senior Director will be responsible for all our Modeling, Analytics, and Business Intelligence Platforms and Products. This leader is responsible for defining the vision, investment priorities, and operating model for platforms that enable data-driven decision making, model development, AI innovation, and enterprise reporting. Working across Technology, Data, Risk, Finance, and business functions, this role drives product strategy, platform modernization, adoption, and value realization while ensuring solutions meet enterprise standards for security, governance, scalability, and regulatory compliance
You will shape how models are built, deployed, consumed, and operationalized across the enterprise - ensuring analytics platforms are easy to use, cost‑effective, secure, and aligned with regulatory and governance expectations.

Product Strategy & Portfolio Leadership
Define strategy, roadmap, and investment priorities for analytics, AI, modeling, and BI products.
Align product portfolio to enterprise goals and business outcomes.
Business Intelligence & Analytics Enablement
Lead enterprise BI, reporting, and self-service analytics capabilities.
Expand access to trusted data, insights, and decision-support tools.
Product Delivery & Value Realization
Oversee product delivery, adoption, and lifecycle management.
Optimize business value, usage, and customer satisfaction.
Stakeholder & Governance Leadership
Partner with business, tech, data, and risk leaders on product priorities.
Ensure products meet governance, security, and regulatory standards.
People Leadership
Lead and develop high-performing product management teams.
Foster a culture of innovation, accountability, and customer focus.
