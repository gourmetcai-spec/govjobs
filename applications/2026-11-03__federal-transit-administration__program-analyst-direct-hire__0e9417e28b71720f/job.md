## Summary

This is a public notice for FTA authorized Program Analyst (0343) positions open to U.S. Citizens. This position is in the FTA Office of Regional Services (TRS). The candidate will be a member of a small team reporting to the Deputy Associate Administrator and will assist in facilitating effective coordination between Headquarters and Regional Offices and support the effective and efficient delivery of regional services and operations.

## Duties

As a Program Analyst, you will:
Serve as a Program Analyst and advisor to management on the evaluation of the effectiveness of government programs. Interprets and applies federal regulations governed by the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) to manage project budgets, audit variances, and enforce strict cost accounting principles.
Act as the primary liaison to key partner offices, capturing and synthesizing external materials to support seamless TRS day-to-day and overseeing high-impact special projects. Provide authoritative guidance to multi-disciplinary staff regarding strict formatting, structural design, and baseline content expectations for complex program analyses and official documentation.
Apply expert qualitative and quantitative methods to collect, analyze, consolidate, and track program performance data, metrics, and status reports to evaluate resource impacts, program efficiency, and compliance with statutory goals.
Evaluate and improve administrative frameworks by designing, developing, and recommending standardized processes for recurring regional operations to enhance program execution and streamline HQ-to-region communications.
Coordinate agency-wide data collection and activity inputs across all geographic regional offices; systematically identifies and leverages opportunities for regional cross-collaboration, knowledge sharing, and improved business process delivery.
The ideal candidate for this position is motivated, experienced and self-reliant professional with exceptional analytical abilities, and effective communication and customer service skills.

## Qualifications

To meet the minimum qualifications for this position, you must meet either the education OR experience qualifications for the grade at which you are requesting consideration and provide a copy of transcripts for verification.
To qualify for the GS-11 on Experience, you must have at least one year of experience equal or equivalent to the GS-09 it must include:
Experience gathering background information on operational planning and organizational excellence initiatives; monitoring processes to enhance program delivery; AND
Experience collecting and consolidating program data for status reports used to evaluate resource impacts, measure goal achievement, and monitor compliance with established organizational goals and improve program oversight; AND
Experience assisting in the development of procurement documentation, including statements of objectives (SOOs), performance requirements, statements of work, and supporting cost estimates for program projects; assisting with tracking contractor performance, deliverables, budgets, and ensuring Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) compliance.
To qualify for the GS-11 on Education alone, you must have:
3 years of progressively higher-level graduate education leading to a Ph.D. degree or Ph.D. or equivalent doctoral degree.
You can also qualify based on a combination of higher-level graduate education and experience. This must be fully supported by your resume and transcripts, provided with your application.
To qualify for the GS-12 on Experience, you must have at least one year of experience equal or equivalent to the GS-11 it must include:
Experience gathering background information, identifying issues, and preparing draft options and recommendations for review by the executive leadership on operational planning and organizational excellence initiatives; monitoring and assisting in implementing improvements for processes to enhance program delivery; AND
Experience collecting, consolidating, and tracking program data for status reports used to evaluate resource impacts, measure goal achievement, and monitor compliance with established organizational goals and improve program oversight; AND
Experience managing special projects of broad organizational impact; to include, developing statements of objectives (SOO), managing contractor performance, deliverables, budgets, and ensuring Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) compliance.
KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES (KSAs): Your qualifications will be evaluated on the basis of your level of knowledge, skills, abilities and/or competencies in the following areas:
Operational Planning - Organizes work, sets priorities, and determines resource requirements; determines short- or long-term goals and strategies to achieve them; coordinates with other organizations or parts of the organization to accomplish goals; monitors progress and evaluates outcomes.
Data Analysis - Knowledge of collecting, consolidating, and tracking complex status reports and data streams from diverse sources and the ability to perform rigorous analyses that support improved program consistency, delivery, and oversight across the organization in collaboration with internal and external offices to translate data into actionable compliance strategies.
Communications - Knowledge of production, and dissemination of operational and informative information and ideas to inform all levels of audience via written, oral, and visual media.
Contract Management - Knowledge of developing detailed statements of objectives that effectively support service programs and projects and the ability to critically assess contractor proposals, action plans, and deliverables to ensure absolute alignment with contractual goals, to include evaluating contractor performance and financial data.
For all types of consideration, experience refers to paid and unpaid experience, including volunteer work done through National Service programs (e.g., Peace Corps, AmeriCorps) and other organizations (e.g., professional, philanthropic, religious, spiritual, community, student, social).  Volunteer work helps build critical competencies, knowledge, and skills and can provide valuable training and experience that translates directly to paid employment. You will receive credit for all qualifying experience, including volunteer experience

This is a public notice for FTA authorized Program Analyst (0343) positions open to U.S. Citizens. This position is in the FTA Office of Regional Services (TRS). The candidate will be a member of a small team reporting to the Deputy Associate Administrator and will assist in facilitating effective coordination between Headquarters and Regional Offices and support the effective and efficient delivery of regional services and operations.
