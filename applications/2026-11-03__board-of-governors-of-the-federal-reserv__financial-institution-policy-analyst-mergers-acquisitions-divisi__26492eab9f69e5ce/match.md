Description of Role
This position offers the rare opportunity to shape financial regulatory policy while working alongside the Federal Reserve's senior leadership on matters of national economic significance. You will work directly with the Vice Chair for Supervision, the Board of Governors, and the senior leadership team of the Supervision & Regulation (S&R) division in a high-impact role at the heart of financial regulatory policy.
The position is located in the Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A) section within the Division of Supervision and Regulation at the Board of Governors (Board). The M&A section oversees the review of applications and notices regarding U.S. banks, bank holding companies, savings and loan holding companies, foreign banks with operations in the U.S., and international operations of U.S. banking organizations, under the relevant statutes and regulations.
As a member of the team, you will develop and implement comprehensive legal strategies for merger and acquisition activities affecting supervised financial institutions, ensuring alignment with the Federal Reserve's regulatory mission and policy objectives. You’ll work collaboratively with the Board’s legal team and the broader S&R team on bank merger and acquisition applications and contribute to the comprehensive review, revision, and updating of internal M&A procedures manuals and related application forms, with a focus on publishing materials to enhance transparency and public accountability of bank M&A application processing policies and procedures. While bank M&A work constitutes the primary responsibility, the position also provides opportunities to contribute to various strategic initiatives supporting the Division Director and Deputy Directors. These initiatives may include policy analysis, research support, and process improvement projects aligned with the Division's strategic objectives.
Specific Job Tasks
Collaborate with individuals and teams across the S&R division and the Board of Governors legal team to process bank M&A applications in an efficient and accurate manner.
Provide written analysis and recommendations on application related matters to management and members of the Board.
Draft communications to the public and the Board about the processing of applications and preparing talking points and preparatory materials for senior leaders and Governors.
Provide expertise and contribute to any changes to the Board’s application-related regulations and guidance.
Preferred Qualifications
Demonstrated expertise in M&A, securities law and corporate governance
Experience in managing complex M&A transactions, preferably financial sector
Strong understanding of corporate and securities laws, regulations, and industry standards
Knowledge of financial institution operations, supervisory processes and comprehensive, consolidated supervision
Knowledge of laws and regulations for bank holding companies, state member banks and foreign banking organizations
Enhanced skill in communicating concepts, ideas and recommendations, both orally and in writing, to senior leaders
Demonstrated sound and mature judgment as well as tact and diplomacy
Demonstrated skill in leading and implementing a variety of projects with cross-functional teams while meeting competing deadlines
A writing sample may be requested.
This position is in Washington, DC and requires an on-site presence.
